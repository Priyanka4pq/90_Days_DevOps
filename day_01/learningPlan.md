## My Understanding of DevOps & Cloud Engineering

DevOps is a culture and set of practices that brings development and operations together to deliver software faster, more reliably, and with automation. Cloud Engineering focuses on designing, deploying, and managing applications and infrastructure on cloud platforms like AWS.

I believe a DevOps engineer should understand infrastructure, automation, monitoring, security, CI/CD, containers, and cloud services—not just individual tools.
